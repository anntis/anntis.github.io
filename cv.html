M.Sc. Economics

University of Bonn

Grade: 2.2 (UK equivalent: 2:1)

Master thesis: "Migration Policy and Environmental Impact: Comparing Double Machine Learning and Bayesian Inference"

More details

The study explores whether migration policies influence municipal waste levels in EU countries, using Double Machine Learning (DML) and Double Robust (DR) Bayesian as base models to estimate the policy's effect. It focuses on the Global Compact for Migration as a measure of a country's openness to migration. Results show that countries adopting the policy tend to produce more municipal waste per capita. DML, following the Correlated Random Effects (CRE) method, offered more reliable estimates by correcting for bias, and non-frequentist (Bayesian) models, including DR Bayesian, proved capable of tackling the problem and producing statistically significant results. Here is the Python DoubleML repository with a small contribution from me.
