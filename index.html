About

Hello, I'm Anna. I'm an economist based in Bonn with an M.Sc. in Economics from the University of Bonn and a bachelor's degree from the Faculty of Statistics at HSE University in Moscow.

I work with data in Python, R and SQL, and build dashboards in Power BI and Looker. Most recently I worked on growth and strategy as an intern at Trivago in Düsseldorf.

Alongside my studies, I worked for three years as a manager in marketing and media for a football club. I really enjoy working with people and learning new things!
