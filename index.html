About

Hello, I'm Anna. I'm an economist based in Bonn with an M.Sc. in Economics from the University of Bonn and a bachelor's degree in Statistics from HSE University in Moscow.

I love everything about data analysis, especially uncovering simple yet non-trivial gems in how things are related to each other. And of course, I enjoy building beautiful graphs and comprehensive dashboards. Python has been with me since the 8th grade, so I mostly trust it to handle the work.

Alongside my studies, I worked for three years as a marketing and media manager for a football club. I thrive on working with people and trying new things!
